The talking-head clipper

Long takes in. Postable clips out.

Turn one recording into clips ready for the feed. Let Claude make the first cut, edit it by changing the words, or fix a line in your own voice.

Get VoxClip ↓See how it works ↓

Free and open source · macOS and Windows

01 / The first cut

Claude does it all.

Drop in a long take and click once. Claude picks the strongest moments, cuts on word boundaries, removes fillers and dead air, and gives each clip a caption style, a cover, a title and post text.

01 Find the opening
Claude can use sampled frames to open on a clear look, follow your face in portrait or square, and blur screens showing text.
02 Add automatic effects
Let Claude place zooms, punch-ins, animated titles, lower thirds and emoji on the words they fit.
03 Keep the final say
Review what changed and undo individual choices. Set a spending cap before any paid work.

02 / Make it yours

Change the words.
Keep your voice.

Edit / Rewrite

Say it again in your own voice.

Delete words in the transcript to cut the video. If you got a number wrong, use Rewrite to replace the line in your cloned voice. On Mac, revoice the whole clip for a single continuous take and use lip sync to match the picture to the new audio.

Style / Export

Give it the right look and sound.

Choose captions that stay clear of feed controls, add music under your voice, and try different colour looks. Export a portrait, square or landscape MP4 with a cover and loudness matched to the feed at −14 LUFS.

Before you post

Check the finished clip.

Claude mode checks its exports on your machine. It listens again to compare the spoken words with your edit and checks sync, loudness and caption placement.

03 / Your setup

Your machine. Your own keys.

Choose your AI.

Use your Claude Code or Codex sign-in, bring a provider API key, or run a local model. Cloud AI receives the transcript and, for plans that use the picture, sampled frames.

See what gets sent.

Voice cloning sends approved voice samples to ElevenLabs; generated speech sends the words and context. Lip sync sends the selected video and audio to fal.ai. Use your own keys and see the price before paid steps.

Work locally.

Transcribe with local Whisper after a model download, or opt into cloud transcription that sends audio to the provider. Keys are stored in Keychain on Mac or encrypted for your Windows account. No telemetry.
