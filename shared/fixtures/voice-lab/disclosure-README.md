# Disclosure interval vectors

`disclosure-intervals.json` is generated by `tools/fixtures/voice-lab/generate_disclosure.py`, independently of either Core. Replay it in both platforms.

The named 42-second case is §15.8's `[[0,3],[11.6,14.7]]` vector. The two gap cases measure **expanded label intervals**, as §11.1 specifies: after the half-second expansion, gaps of 0.9 seconds merge and gaps of 1.1 seconds remain separate. Their original segment gaps are 1.9 and 2.1 seconds. A raw segment gap of 1.1 seconds still merges after padding; treating it as a separate label would contradict §11.1. The final case clamps padding at the clip end.
