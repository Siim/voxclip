# ADR-R37: pooled voice checks for short generated phrases

Date: 2026-11-02. Status: accepted lead decision.

The casual-speech replay spent 39 synthesis requests on 13 phrases and rejected every take before checking words. Raw lengths were 1.36–3.12 seconds; trimming and VAD left less than the calibrated three seconds of voiced speech. A local transcript review found the intended words in 37 takes and “fight simulator” in two. Test audio is synthetic; no user recording is stored.

Every completed take receives the existing Scribe word comparison, clipping and seam-loudness checks within the approved quote. A take already rejected by generated-speaker extraction skips Scribe and records only its synthesis spend. Five tokens or fewer still require exact words. Longer generated takes with at least 48,000 voiced samples at 16 kHz keep their own speaker check.

For shorter takes, collect generated VAD output in take order for the same consent record. Split each approved run into consecutive groups, closing each at the first take that reaches 48,000 voiced samples. A leftover tail below that minimum joins the previous group. Check the groups after the last take, and check the completed subset on Stop. Use the existing 48,000-sample windows, 24,000-sample hops, consent embedding and threshold. Never add source speech, padding, repetition or an extra paid request. A measured group mismatch adds the speaker failure only to that group’s takes; it does not erase a separate words failure.

Try another pools the new short take with earlier generated voiced samples from this plan, preferring that phrase's history most recent first, then other phrases most recent first. Stop borrowing as soon as 48,000 samples are available, truncating the final donor to the needed samples. Donor takes keep their previous verdicts. No source speech or current take is reused.

Any measured speaker mismatch for a consent record, including a long take or a group, blocks the unmeasured exception throughout the plan. A passing measurement in the same approved run cannot erase it. A later approved run must pass a measured check to clear the block. Apply that block to earlier unmeasured takes too; another consent record has independent state.

Until the pool finishes, rows say “waiting for the voice check” and cannot be selected. Once per phrase, a passing checked short take says “Short phrase · words checked · voice checked across 3 takes” with the actual count. If the completed pool still has fewer than 48,000 voiced samples, say “voice couldn't be measured (under 3 s of speech)”. Such a row is selectable only for an active own clone with recordedStatement or projectAttestation consent, and only after its words and acoustic checks pass. Record the unmeasured evidence explicitly, with no invented speaker score or passed measurement. Unmeasured takes require an explicit user click; automatic selection considers only measured passing takes and preserves any valid explicit selection. Final voice checks preserve specific fatal/provider errors and name consent failures truthfully.

The first run quotes one candidate per phrase. Try another separately quotes and approves one more synthesis plus its bounded Scribe check. Prices come from the held plan; the three visible take slots remain. Speech estimates use a provider rate floor, trim allowances and a voiced-fraction margin as an advisory for optional surrounding-sentence review; short phrases are never forced to expand.

Existing measured recipes retain their format. Optional short-speech evidence (`recordId`, matching `consentRecordId`) preserves pooled checks or the own-clone under-length exception through acceptance and rendering. Existing frozen shared replay fixtures are unchanged. Windows adoption of the new unmeasured rendering policy remains a separate platform gate.
